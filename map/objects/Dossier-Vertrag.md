# Dossier-Vertrag

The Praxis-Check output contract. It reframes the dossier as Kernbefund-first, evidence-attributed rather than advice, with a tangential-endpoint exclusion, so the answer reads as 'what the literature says' and never as 'what to do'. This is the textual half of staying on the library side of the regulatory line.

binds [[Praxis-Check]] . binds [[Synthesize]] . governed-by [[Regulatory Boundary]]

- **Hits:** [[Praxis-Check]] dossier shape and the framing a clinician reads. Whether an answer stays bibliographic.
- **Does not hit:** the [[Brief Contract]]. The Vertrag shapes the on-demand dossier; the Brief Contract shapes the weekly Wochenbrief. Two different artifacts, two contracts.
