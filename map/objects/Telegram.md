# Telegram

The weekly delivery channel. The brief lands here, split at paper boundaries, once a week, no dashboard to check. Also the intended (unbuilt) capture point for her reactions in the feedback loop.

receives-from [[Send]] . reaches [[The Clinician]] . would-feed [[Feedback Stage]]

- **Hits:** [[Send]] formatting (the 4096-char split targets Telegram). Where the clinician actually reads the brief.
- **Does not hit:** the [[Web Seam]]. Telegram is the weekly push channel; the web layer is the on-demand pull surface. Different artifacts reach her by different doors.
