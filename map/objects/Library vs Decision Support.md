# Library vs Decision Support

The regulatory pull. Kustos is most useful to the clinician the closer it gets to 'what should I do for this patient', but that is exactly the MDCG line that turns a library into a medical device under the MDR and the high-risk AI Act, the step that expelled OpenEvidence from the EU. The guardrails and the Vertrag hold it on the low-value, legal side on purpose. Both sides live: the pull toward usefulness and the pull toward staying legal are always present at once.

- **Hits:** every framing decision and the product's legal existence. It is why the dossier is Kernbefund-first and evidence-attributed rather than advisory.
- **Does not hit:** the retrieval code. You can cross this line without changing a line of code, by changing only the declared intended purpose. The tension is about description, not implementation.
